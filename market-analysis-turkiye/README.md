# Türkiye Market Analysis: Regional & Industry & Finally Company Selection Strategy

> **Project Link:** [View full interactive project on scdata.ai](https://www.scdata.ai/project/78264)

## Objective
This project analyzes business performance metrics across regions and industries in Türkiye to identify optimal sectors for enterprise entry, investment, or strategic operations by evaluating profitability, cost structures, and revenue concentration.

---

## Analysis Steps

### 1. Regional Revenue Distribution

<img width="1463" alt="Region by Revenue" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d37fdfa0-7b1d-42a7-adc4-186d778f95f3" />

* **Metropolitan Dominance:** Approximately eight-ninths (~86.5%) of analyzed enterprises are concentrated in three major metropolitan hubs: İstanbul, Ankara, and İzmir.
* **İstanbul's Lead:** İstanbul significantly outperforms all other regions in median net income per enterprise, with Ankara holding a distant second place.

---

### 2. Profit vs. Cost Dynamics by Industry

<img width="1447" alt="Profit vs Cost" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e1cd2a97-27ec-4e9d-b5cd-55d05cdc04cd" />

* While the **revenue-to-cost regression slope** across all industries ranges slightly above parity ($1.01$ to $1.28$), the **profit-to-cost slope** reveals extremely thin operating margins, varying strictly between $0.02$ and $0.14$.
* The highest profit slope ($0.14$) belongs to the Real Estate sector (notably led by EKGYO).

---

### 3. Sector Breakdown & Performance

| Industry | Revenue Level | Net Income | Profitability | Market Structure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy** | High (2nd) | High (3rd) | High (2nd) | Balanced growth & high capacity |
| **Real Estate** | Moderate | High (1st) | High (4th) | Concentrated (A few giants, long tail of small firms) |
| **Financials** | High (1st) | Low | Moderate (3rd) | Highly skewed (Large volume, lower median margins) |
| **Industrials** | Above Average | Low | Low | High total revenue, low bottom-line conversion |
| **Utilities** | Moderate | Negative | Low | Unprofitable sector-wide net margins |
| **Healthcare & IT** | Low | Low | Low | Currently underdeveloped in total revenue share |

---

### 4. Company-Level Analysis within the Energy Sector

<img width="1481" alt="Company Comparison" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/665222ed-18c9-49d7-b3d4-2cede37251d0" />

* **Market Leader vs. Competitors:** Revenue leader **Koç Holding** demonstrates strong economies of scale, maintaining optimized cost structures and superior margins across operating and net income tiers.
* **Financial Leverage Impact:** **Naturelgaz** shows competitive revenue generation, but high debt service costs / financial leverage depress its Earnings Before Tax (EBT).
* **SG&A Overhead:** **İpek Doğal Enerji** suffers from disproportionately high SG&A (Selling, General & Administrative) expenses, erosion-inducing to its bottom line.
* **Scale Filter:** The remaining smaller players lack the revenue volume required for a high-capacity, high-volume market presence.

---

## 5. Final Strategic Recommendation

**Primary Target Companies — Koç Holding or Naturelgaz:**
* **Koç Holding** is the optimal choice due to its market dominance, superior cost optimization, and overall margin resilience.
* **Naturelgaz** stands as a strong secondary option for market entry, provided its financial leverage is restructured.
* **İpek Doğal Enerji** and low-volume peers are excluded due to structural SG&A drags and inadequate market scale.

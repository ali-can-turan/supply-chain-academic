# Enterprise Diagnosis & Value Driver Analysis: KİRAÇ Inc. (BIST: TCKRC)

> **Interactive Project:** [View full analysis on scdata.ai (Project #78402)](https://www.scdata.ai/project/78402)

## Executive Summary
This enterprise diagnosis evaluates **KİRAÇ Inc. (BIST: TCKRC)** within Türkiye's Industrials sector (Building Products sub-industry). By comparing the top 5 revenue holders alongside KİRAÇ, the analysis uncovers a striking **volume-versus-profitability anomaly**: while industry giants struggle with compressed margins due to elevated $COGS$ and SG&A drag, KİRAÇ achieves top-tier net profit margins through superior cost structure and operating efficiency.

* **The Efficiency Frontier:** **KİRAÇ** positions itself at the top-left apex of the efficiency frontier, combining low $\frac{COGS}{\text{Revenue}}$ ratios with high net income margins.
* **Peer Group Drag:** Competitors such as **ALARKO** and **BIEN** cluster towards the right end of the spectrum, where inflated $COGS$ consumption severely penalizes bottom-line conversion.

### 3. Enterprise Ranking — Net Income Disparity (in $M)

<img width="1300" alt="Enterprise Ranking" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ac24cf06-d1bc-4cd7-a42b-0bc2e8db0586" />

* **The Margin Anomaly:** **KİRAÇ**—whose total revenue is roughly **1/75th** that of industry leader TÜRKİYE ŞİŞE CAM—ranks **second in absolute net earnings**, generating nearly **1/8th** of ŞİŞE's total bottom line.
* **Strategic Takeaway:** This sharp divergence proves that top-line volume does not guarantee shareholder value. KİRAÇ's hyper-lean cost structure allows it to convert revenue into actual net earnings far more efficiently than mega-cap competitors.

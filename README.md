## Executive Overview

This portfolio bridges **Supply Chain Operations** and **Corporate Finance**, establishing a top-down analytical methodology that connects macro-sectoral exposure to micro-firm financial diagnostic modeling. 

Rather than treating supply chain metrics ($DIO$, $CCC$, $DPO$, $DSO$) in isolation, this work evaluates how inventory deployment and procurement strategies directly impact balance sheet health, asset productivity ($ROA$), capital structure ($LAR$), and shareholder value.

## Project Summaries & Analytical Highlights

### Project 01: Türkiye Market Analysis — Regional & Industry Selection Strategy
* Analyzed regional concentration (88.9% revenue in top 3 hubs) and thin industry profit-to-cost slopes ($0.02$–$0.14$).
* Selected **Energy** (Koç Holding / Naturelgaz) as the optimal sector, balancing scale with margin resilience while avoiding financial yield risks.

### Project 02: Competitive Landscape & Financial Health Analysis — Shell plc
* Validated the oligopolistic profit frontier where Shell and BP control ~85% of sector operating income.
* Showed Shell outperforming BP by ~20% in operating margin by offsetting high $DIO$ with supplier financing ($DPO$) and brand leverage ($DSO$).

### Project 03: Enterprise Diagnosis & Value Driver Analysis — KİRAÇ Inc. (BIST: TCKRC)
* Uncovered KİRAÇ's profitability anomaly: generating 1/8th of ŞİŞECAM's net bottom line at just 1/75th of its revenue scale.
* Proved that lean SG&A and $COGS$ management yields superior net margin conversion over overhead-heavy market leaders.

### Project 04: Cross-Country & Sectoral Inventory Analysis — China, Türkiye, and USA
* Resolved the US Healthcare $DIO$ paradox, proving high holding periods are diluted on balance sheets by massive **Net Intangible Assets**.
* Demonstrated that Turkish industrial firms utilize inventory as an **inflation hedge** and a safeguard against high fixed-cost assembly line halts ($PPE$).

### Project 05: Comprehensive Global Sourcing Analysis — EGEPRO Polyethylene Procurement
* Designed a multi-criteria procurement model selecting **China** (#1 with 10 pts) over Japan, India, and Indonesia.
* Formulated a **dual-sourcing strategy**: allocating primary volume to **Zhejiang** (34.5 pts) backed by **Sinofibers** (33.5 pts) for buyer leverage ($BPI$).

## Core Competencies & Methodological Frameworks

* **Financial & Supply Chain Integration:** Connecting working capital metrics ($DIO$, $DSO$, $DPO$, $CCC$) with corporate financial drivers ($ROA$, $ROE$, $LAR$, $EBT$).
* **Macro-to-Micro Diagnostic Pipeline:** Executing top-down market screening from country-level trade dynamics down to firm-level financial statements.
* **Strategic Procurement Modeling:** Constructing Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) matrices, evaluating Buyer Power Indices ($BPI$), and establishing dual-sourcing frameworks.
* **Macroeconomic Risk Mitigation:** Modeling inventory asset behavior as an inflationary hedge and evaluating operational line-halt risks versus inventory carrying costs.

# Comprehensive Global Sourcing Analysis: Polyethylene Raw Material

> **Buyer Country:** Türkiye  
> **Buyer Firm:** Ege Profil Ticaret ve Sanayi A.Ş. (BIST: EGEPRO)  
> **Target Product:** Plastic Pipe  
> **Key Raw Material:** Polyethylene

> **Interactive Project Reference:** [View full analysis on scdata.ai (Project #78757)](https://www.scdata.ai/project/78757)

---

## Executive Summary
This global sourcing study establishes a Polyethylene procurement framework for **Ege Profil (EGEPRO)**.

* **Macro Country Selection:** China ranked #1 (**10 pts**) among 5 candidate markets, outperforming Japan (9 pts) and India/Indonesia (6 pts each) across market concentration, trade barriers, FX stability, and $LPI$ scores.
* **Micro Supplier Benchmarking:** 5 Chinese Polyethylene suppliers were scored across 17 financial/operational metrics. **Zhejiang** (**34.5 pts**) and **Sinofibers** (**33.5 pts**) emerged as top performers.
* **Strategic Decision:** Implement a **dual-sourcing model**—allocating primary volume to Zhejiang for overall balance sheet and asset strength, while leveraging Sinofibers for R&D capacity and buyer bargaining leverage ($BPI$).

## 3. Strategic Sourcing Recommendation

1. **Primary Target — ZHEJIANG (34.5 pts):** Ranks #1 overall, backed by strong asset turnover ($FAT$), consistent earnings growth, high $ROA$, and solid net tangible assets.
2. **Secondary Target — SINOFIBERS (33.5 pts):** Strong runner-up with superior R&D investment, higher liquidity/solvency buffers ($CR/QR$, $1/LAR$), and favorable buyer bargaining leverage ($BPI = 2$).
3. **Action Plan:** Launch simultaneous commercial negotiations with **Zhejiang** and **Sinofibers** to execute a competitive dual-sourcing allocation strategy.
